Which oil goes where, and how. Written for my vata prakriti, mostly with the sinus in mind.[^consult] Pitta entries are from general references.

## Basics

- Warm oil. Cold oil aggravates vata. Kapha takes it warm to hot, pitta lukewarm.
- Vata is dry, light, cold, rough, mobile. Heavy, warm, generous oil.
- Kapha is heavy, slow, cold, oily, dense. The least oil that works: light, hot, brisk.
- Pitta is hot, sharp, a little oily. Cooling oils, moderate amounts.
- Vata and kapha are both cold, so warming oils serve both. Vata takes it heavy and generous, kapha in the smallest amount that works.

## Abhyanga (body massage)

- Vata
	- **Sesame oil** as the base.
	- **Almond oil** for face and head. Lighter than sesame.
	- **Mahanarayana taila** mixed into the sesame. Joints, stiffness.
	- **Dhanwantharam taila**. Rejuvenating, supports the nervous system, relieves fatigue.
	- **Bala taila** or **Bala Ashwagandhadi taila** for thinness and fatigue.
	- **Ksheerabala taila** for anxiety, insomnia, nerve pain.
	- **Sahacharadi taila** for legs and sciatica. **Karpasasthyadi taila** for neck and shoulders.
	- **Castor oil** is very heavy. One part in four of sesame.
	- **Ghee** for dryness that comes with heat or irritation.
- Kapha
	- **Kottamchukkadi taila**, **Nirgundi taila**, or plain **mustard oil**.
	- **Udvartana**, dry powder massage with **Kolakulathadi**, instead of oil when heaviness is the issue.
	- **Prasarini taila** for cold joints. **Karpasasthyadi** works here too.
	- **Coconut oil** and **ghee** don't suit kapha.
- Pitta
	- **Coconut oil** as the base.
	- **Sunflower oil** as a light base. **Chandanadi taila** for burning, heat, restless sleep.
	- **Ksheerabala** and **Dhanwantharam** are tolerated. Sesame-based but gentle.
	- Lukewarm oil. Mustard doesn't suit pitta.

## Hair and scalp

- Vata
	- **Sesame oil**, **almond oil**.
	- **Bhringraj taila**. Roots the hair, prevents premature hair fall, calms vata-type sleep issues.
	- **Bala Ashwagandhadi taila**. Strengthens weak hair roots, relieves mental stress.
	- **Neelibhringadi taila** or **Brahmi Bhringadi taila** for hair fall.
- Kapha
	- **Mustard oil**.
	- A light base with **neem**, **rosemary** or **tulsi**. Scalp only.
- Pitta
	- **Coconut oil** base. **Neelibhringadi** for hair fall and early greying.
	- **Brahmi taila** on the head for sleep and stress.

## Nasya (nose)

- **Anu taila**. The traditional medicated oil for daily or therapeutic nasya. Clears the senses, grounds the nervous system.
- Helps with sinus and nasal blockage.[^sinus]
- **Almond oil** on the face and head also helps the sinus.
- Warm the oil, head tilted back, two drops per nostril, sniff gently. Forceful inhaling can pull oil into the airway (lipoid pneumonia).
- Vata: **Anu taila** daily. **Shadbindu taila** for headaches. **Ksheerabala 101** for anxiety.
- Kapha: **Shadbindu**, **Vacha taila**. Not ghee.
- Pitta: **ghee**, or **Anu taila** at the two-drop dose. Skip during fever or inflammation.

## Ear oiling

- Warm **sesame** or **Ksheerabala**, weekly, for vata.
- Skip with any congestion or fluid in the ear.

## How to apply

| | Vata | Kapha | Pitta |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amount | Generous, more than feels necessary | A thin film | Moderate |
| Pressure and speed | Slow, steady, long strokes | Brisk, vigorous | Even, unhurried |
| How often | Daily, or 3 to 4 times a week | 1 to 2 times a week, dry massage more often | 2 to 3 times a week |
| Leave it on | 20 to 30 minutes | 10 to 15 minutes | 15 to 20 minutes |
| Afterwards | Warm shower, little soap, keep a film | Hot shower, wash it off | Lukewarm shower |
| Time of day | Morning, or before bed | Morning | Morning or evening, not midday |
| Where to focus | Feet and scalp | Chest, back, joints. Light on a congested chest | Head, feet, palms |
| Oil temperature | Warm | Warm to hot | Lukewarm |

## Cautions

- Skip abhyanga during fever, illness, indigestion and right after eating.
- Patch-test anything new. Several of these are potent and heating.
- Check with a practitioner before regular nasya if pregnant, on medication, or with a skin or sinus condition.
- **Kottakkal**, **AVP** and **Vaidyaratnam** are the reliable brands.

    - banana
